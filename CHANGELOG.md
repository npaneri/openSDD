## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-31

### Added

- Full SpecSet-1 closeout: all 10 phases completed, methodology proven across three SpecSets
- `bin/sdd` CLI wrapper with `init`, `phase`, `status` subcommands
- `SKILL.md` agent-skill entry point for skill-aware agents
- `docs/ai-compatibility.md`: AI compatibility tool matrix and invocation mappings
- `docs/stores.md`: cross-repo shared-spec guidance with three adoption patterns
- `catalog/` with role-based phase-cycle variants (PM, developer, QA, architect)
- `extensions/` mechanism: convention + worked example template
- `examples/minimal-specset/`: complete toy SpecSet built purely from templates
- `sdd/templates/bug-fix.template.md` and `sdd/templates/idea-assessment.template.md`: optional extension templates
- Initial toolkit import: 5 stage guides (`sdd/01`-`05`), 6 templates (`sdd/templates/`)
- Repo scaffolding: `README.md`, `LICENSE`, `.gitignore`, `CHANGELOG.md` seeded at `0.1.0`
- SpecSet-1 roadmap with 10 sequential phases
- `brainstorm_ideas.md` with settled decisions
